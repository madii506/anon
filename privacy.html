what anon stores

Nothing

No database of users, keys or trades. The server builds transactions on request and forgets them. Keys, labels and your launch list live in this browser's storage.

what breaks the link

A fresh key with a clean first funder

A key made here has no history. Fund it from an exchange or through a route and nothing on-chain points back to your main wallet.

the limits

What privacy here can't do.

It can't hide the trade

Every launch and swap is public on Solana. The key is anonymous, its activity is not.

It can't fix a dirty funder

Send to a fresh key from your main wallet and the link exists forever.

It can't stop timing

A withdrawal and a buy of the same size minutes apart can be paired.

It can't recover keys

Clear this browser's storage without an export and the key is gone for good.

It can't hide your IP from the services

Use your own network protections if that matters to you.

It can't promise pump.fun's uploads

Launches use pump.fun's image upload. If it stops answering, launches pause and the page says so.

the change

pump.fun, November 30.

pump.fun ends external wallet sign-in on its website on November 30 at 15:00 UTC and moves to email, Google, Apple and GitHub accounts. Keeping an existing account means importing the wallet's private key, which is encrypted in the browser and held by its login provider, Privy. Funds and trading are not affected, according to the reports. Critics, including former Mt. Gox CEO Mark Karpelès, say the security question moves from who holds the key to who can log in.
