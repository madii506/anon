01 — the change

pump.fun is turning off wallet login.

On November 30 at 15:00 UTC pump.fun stops letting you sign in on its website with Phantom, Solflare or Ledger. You sign in with email, Google, Apple or GitHub instead. To keep an old account, the migration guide asks you to import your wallet's private key, which their login provider holds encrypted.

Not your keys, not your crypto. anon goes the other way: there is nothing to sign in to, and the key never leaves your browser.

11 — honest limits

What anon can't hide.

public chain

Everything on Solana is public

A fresh key hides who you are, not what the key does. Its trades are on-chain like anyone's.

funding

Fund it from your main wallet and the link is back

Send from an exchange, or route it in, so the first funder isn't your own address.

timing

Timing and amounts can match up

A deposit followed by a buy of the same size minutes later can be paired by anyone watching.

storage

Clear your browser, lose the key

Keys live in this browser's storage. Export a copy. Nobody, including us, can recover one.

no custody

We can't undo a mistake

anon never holds your funds or your keys, so it can't refund a wrong send either.

fees

Network fees still apply

Solana fees, pump.fun's fees and any route fee are shown before you sign. anon adds none of its own.

13 — questions

Asked and answered.

Is anon a wallet?

The key is the wallet. It lives in this browser, you can export it to a file, and you can burn it. There is no app, no extension and no account.

Can anon see my private key?

No. Keys are made with your browser's own WebCrypto and stored in its local storage. The server only receives your public address and, when you trade, transactions your browser already signed.

How do I launch a coin?

Open Launch, make or pick a key, send it a little SOL, add an image, a name and a ticker, and press Launch. pump.fun's IPFS stores the metadata, PumpPortal builds the create transaction, and your browser signs it with your key and the new coin's mint key.

Do I need a pump.fun account?

No. Coins live on Solana and the transactions go straight to the chain from your key. If pump.fun's image upload ever stops answering, launches pause and the page says so.

Can I still use Phantom or Solflare?

You can send from them to fund a key. You never connect them, so nothing ties them to the page.

How do I keep a key safe?

On Keys, press Export to save it as a file. Anyone with that file controls the funds, so keep it like cash.

Does anon charge a fee?

anon adds no fee. Solana's network fee, pump.fun's own fees and a route's fee, where you use one, still apply and are shown before you sign.
