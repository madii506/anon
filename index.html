    05 — the rules

    What anon never does. And what it can't hide.

    anon never

    • Holds your funds. Routes pay your wallets directly; nothing sits with anon.
    • Sees your keys. Fresh keys are made and kept in your browser only.
    • Asks for a seed phrase. Your main wallet only signs what it shows you.
    • Tracks you. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics.
    • Adds a fee to trades. You pay the network and the route, both shown first.

    honest limits

    • Amounts and timing can match. 1.234 SOL in and 1.233 out a minute later is easy to pair. Use round amounts and wait.
    • The route provider sees its own swap. NEAR Intents knows both ends of a route it fills.
    • Your IP reaches the APIs. Use a VPN if that matters to you.
    • You can undo it. Sending between your main and a fresh wallet links them again.
    06 — faq

    Questions, answered.

    Is anon a mixer?+

    No. anon never pools or holds anyone's funds. A fresh wallet is a new keypair in your browser. A route is a swap filled by NEAR Intents solvers, who pay the destination from their own funds. The privacy comes from a new wallet plus a swap, not from mixing.

    Where does a fresh wallet's key live?+

    Only in this browser's storage. anon's servers never see it. Use export in the safehouse to copy it into Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, and do that before you clear your browser data, which deletes it.

    Do I still need Phantom?+

    Only to fund a fresh wallet from your main one. Once a fresh wallet holds SOL, it trades by itself. While it is on, trades go through without a popup, because the key is in this browser.

    What does it cost?+

    anon adds no fee. A fast hop costs about 0.1% through NEAR Intents, and the shielded route costs a little more across its two legs. Trades pay the Solana network fee and the route's price impact, both shown before you confirm.

    What can I trade from a fresh wallet?+

    Everything in the terminal: 68 tokenized stocks, ETFs and metals (xStocks, not offered to US persons), SOL, BTC and ETH perps through Jupiter, live pump.fun coins, and the city's 24 assets.

    Is anon affiliated with NEAR, Zcash, Jupiter, pump.fun or any game?+

    No. anon is independent. It uses their public APIs and is not endorsed by any of them. Nothing here is financial or legal advice.

    What is $ANON?+

    The city's coin. The terminal works the same whether you hold it or not.
